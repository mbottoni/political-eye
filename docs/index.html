political-eye

Brazilian politics and public money from official open data. Scheduled jobs download each source, rebuild tidy tables and commit them, so the repository's history is a record of what changed and when.

Live

Câmara dos Deputados weekly

Every month since 2015 as two graphs on the same deputies: who votes together and who signs bills together.

Câmara, Dados Abertos

Party orientations weekly

How often each deputy votes the way their party and the government told them to, and with their party's majority. Data and Python for now; no page yet.

Câmara, Dados Abertos

Senado Federal weekly

The same two graphs for the Senate, who votes together and who signs together, each over the twelve months to date, and how often each senator votes with their party and the government.

Senado, Dados Abertos

Parliamentary amendments daily

Every amendment since 2014: who sent it, where, how much was committed and paid, and which organisations received the money, "Pix" transfers included. Data for now; no page yet.

Portal da Transparência (CGU)

Sanctions daily

Companies, organisations and people barred from public contracts or fined for corruption, and the day each entered and left the lists, which CGU itself does not keep.

CGU: CEIS, CNEP, CEPIM, leniency agreements

Campaign finance weekly

Where each candidate's money came from in 2018, 2022 and 2026: party leaderships handing out public funds, large donors, their own pockets, and which companies they paid.

TSE

Municipal finances monthly

Which towns break the legal cap on payroll spending, and how much cash each holds at year end. The history since 2015 is still being read.

Tesouro Nacional: Siconfi

Public contracts daily

Every contract of R$1 million or more, every contract signed with a company then barred by the sanctions lists, and daily totals of everything published. The history since 2021 is still being read.

PNCP

Deputies across sources weekly

One row per deputy: how they vote with their party and the government, the amendments they sent, and the campaign that elected them.
