Party orientations

Before every roll call in the Câmara, each party's leader, the Government's leader and the blocs tell their deputies how to vote. These pages measure who follows: their party, the Government, and the rest of their party. Every share is over the contested Plenário roll calls (those with at least 2.5% on the losing side), from the Câmara's open data, refreshed weekly.

with_party: of the votes where the deputy's party (or its federation or bloc) oriented Sim, Não or Obstrução, the share they followed. with_gov: the same against the Government leader. with_majority: the share where they voted with most of the other deputies of their party who voted, which needs no orientation. Rice index: the average of |yes − no| / (yes + no) within a party across roll calls (1 = always unanimous). Every rule, and the gaps in the Câmara's data (cut-off bloc names from 2023, a bloc with no orientation from February to October 2025), is in methodology/camara.md. Source: Câmara dos Deputados, Dados Abertos.
