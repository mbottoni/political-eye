Sanctions

Who is barred from public contracts or punished for corruption in Brazil, by whom, and for how long. These pages also show what sanctioned companies still receive from the State. They cover the federal government's four lists: CEIS (barred and suspended companies and people), CNEP (penalties under the Anti-Corruption Law), CEPIM (non-profits barred from federal agreements) and the leniency agreements companies signed. CGU publishes each list as a daily snapshot and keeps no history, so political-eye records every day's version.

The lists hold only sanctions still listed. A sanction that expired or was lifted has gone, so older years look smaller than they were, and a company's past is only what is still listed. Sanctioned people are counted but never named on these pages, and their CPF is masked in the data. Matching to contracts, amendments and campaign suppliers is by CNPJ, so only companies and organisations are matched. A match is not by itself an irregularity: most sanctions bind only part of the State. Every rule is in methodology/sancoes.md. Sources: CGU (Portal da Transparência), PNCP, TSE.
